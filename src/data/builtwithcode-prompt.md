# Build a Cinematic 3D Reel Entirely With Code

You are an elite creative director, 3D motion designer, technical artist, and Three.js engineer.

**Your mission:** Create a stunning, production-quality 55–60 second vertical Reel showing how Claude can create an entire animated video using nothing but code.

You are responsible for EVERYTHING: creative direction, storytelling, procedural modeling, animation, cinematography, lighting, transitions, typography, sound design, rendering, and final video export.

**Do not just create a demo. Deliver the finished, polished MP4 video.**

## Concept

**Title: "I Asked Claude to Build This Entire Video With Code"**

The video itself must prove the concept. Everything viewers see should be generated programmatically through Three.js.

Make it feel like a premium motion-design studio produced it, not a generic AI-generated animation.

The experience should be visually surprising, fast-paced, satisfying, and highly engaging.

## Creative Direction

- Style: Premium stylized 3D / 2.5D motion graphics.
- Aesthetic: Clean, modern, dimensional, cinematic, visually sophisticated.
- Inspiration: High-end technology commercials, exceptional motion-design showreels, and polished 3D explainers.
- Use beautiful materials, realistic lighting, soft shadows, ambient occlusion, depth of field, and deliberate color grading.
- Incorporate procedural geometric models, animated mechanical assemblies, miniature environments, and expressive characters where appropriate.
- Favor striking visual composition over complicated clutter.
- Avoid generic AI imagery, purple neon aesthetics, random floating objects, boring slides, and repetitive transitions.

Every scene should look intentional and professionally art-directed.

## Storyboard

Create an approximately 60-second animated sequence:

**Scene 1 — The Hook (0–5 seconds)**

Start with a visually stunning 3D object or composition that immediately grabs attention.

Use a cinematic camera move and a surprising visual transformation.

On-screen message:

"I ASKED CLAUDE TO BUILD THIS VIDEO."

Then reveal:

"ENTIRELY WITH CODE."

The first two seconds must be exceptionally compelling.

**Scene 2 — Code Becomes Geometry (5–15 seconds)**

Show actual code-like structures transforming into real 3D geometry.

Lines become curves, curves become surfaces, and surfaces assemble into detailed three-dimensional objects.

Show beautiful procedural modeling in action.

Use close-up camera shots, satisfying object assembly, smooth morphing, and synchronized sound effects.

**Scene 3 — Building a Character (15–25 seconds)**

Procedurally construct a distinctive stylized 3D character.

Build its components piece by piece, then bring it to life.

Animate it with expressive body language, natural movement, anticipation, follow-through, and personality.

The character must feel intentionally designed, not assembled from generic primitives without artistic refinement.

**Scene 4 — Creating a World (25–38 seconds)**

Transform the environment into an impressive miniature 3D world.

Objects rise, unfold, connect, and assemble into a cohesive scene.

Introduce sophisticated lighting, environmental details, shadows, and an impressive cinematic camera fly-through.

Use a visual reveal that makes viewers want to replay the sequence.

**Scene 5 — Bringing Everything to Life (38–49 seconds)**

Demonstrate how code controls motion, camera movement, lighting, and composition.

Use several highly polished animation moments with impactful timing, seamless transitions, and dimensional movement.

Show visual complexity without sacrificing clarity.

**Scene 6 — The Final Reveal (49–60 seconds)**

Pull the camera back to reveal that the entire animated sequence was constructed from code.

Bring the character, environment, and visual elements together in one stunning hero composition.

End with:

"EVERY FRAME. BUILT WITH CODE."

"THREE.JS × CLAUDE"

Finish with a satisfying visual payoff and seamless loop-friendly transition.

The storyboard is a creative starting point. Improve it whenever doing so produces a stronger video.

## Technical Requirements

Use:

- Three.js as the primary 3D rendering engine.
- TypeScript for implementation.
- Custom shaders where they materially improve visuals.
- Procedural geometry and programmatically generated models.
- Skeletal or hierarchical character animation.
- Physically based materials and professional lighting.
- Smooth cinematic camera movement.
- Carefully crafted motion curves and easing.
- Post-processing when it meaningfully improves image quality.
- FFmpeg to create the final MP4.
- Playwright or another reliable automated frame-capture approach if appropriate.

You may install any additional tools, dependencies, or freely usable assets required to achieve the best result.

All animation and visuals must be controlled through code. Do not substitute AI-generated video clips or manually animated footage.

## Animation Quality

Motion quality is EXTREMELY important.

Implement:

- Smooth object assembly and transformation.
- Natural character movement and secondary motion.
- Camera tracking, orbiting, dolly movements, and dramatic reveals.
- Strong animation timing and visual rhythm.
- Anticipation, overshoot, settling, and follow-through.
- Visually seamless transitions between scenes.
- Carefully synchronized motion, audio, and typography.

Avoid linear robotic animation, arbitrary spinning objects, obvious looping motion, and static compositions.

Every movement should have a reason.

## Audio

Produce a professional voiceover that complements the visuals and tells a compelling story.

Use natural-sounding speech, not robotic narration.

Add background music and carefully synchronized sound effects.

Use only tools and assets that permit the intended published use, including monetization.

Audio should enhance the animation rather than overwhelm it.

## Export Requirements

- Format: MP4, H.264.
- Resolution: 1080 × 1920.
- Aspect ratio: 9:16.
- Frame rate: 30 FPS or 60 FPS.
- Duration: Approximately 55–60 seconds.
- High-quality video encoding.
- Clean, readable animated typography.
- Keep critical content inside safe areas for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.

## Rendering Architecture

Create a deterministic animation timeline that can render any frame at an exact timestamp.

Do not depend on real-time playback speed for video generation.

Render frames in a repeatable process so frame drops or hardware performance cannot change the final animation.

Optimize geometry, materials, lighting, and render settings without sacrificing visible quality.

## Quality Assurance

Do not stop after implementing the code.

Actually run the application, inspect rendered frames, and export the finished video.

Review multiple frames from every scene.

Identify and fix:

- Poor composition.
- Unattractive models.
- Unnatural movement.
- Camera clipping.
- Broken shadows or lighting.
- Unreadable typography.
- Awkward transitions.
- Timing problems.
- Empty or visually boring sections.
- Audio synchronization issues.

Iterate until the result looks like professional motion design.

You have full autonomy over implementation and creative decisions.

Do not ask me to select colors, models, libraries, or animation techniques.

Research and install whatever you need.

Do not sacrifice quality merely to finish faster.

Prioritize, in order:

1. Exceptional visual quality.
2. Sophisticated animation and cinematography.
3. Compelling storytelling and retention.
4. Reliable deterministic rendering.
5. Rendering efficiency.

**The final deliverable must be a fully rendered, publish-ready video, not just a working Three.js project.**

Also deliver the complete source code so the entire video can be regenerated or modified.

Start building immediately.
